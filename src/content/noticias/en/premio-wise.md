Cabañas Alpinas El Limo won first place at the WISE El Salvador Award in the Tourism & Science category.

The award joins other recognition and support for the project, such as the Digital Transition Seed Capital (KfW and the DINÁMICA Initiative), the Seed Capital of the Green SME Development Program (KOICA) and the Ilamatepec Orange Tourism project (European Union and International Trade Centre).
