Cabañas Alpinas El Limo hosts an air quality monitoring station developed with the University of El Salvador (UES) and AireSV.

It tracks the Air Quality Index (AQI), PM2.5 and PM10 particles and CO₂. The readings can be checked in the AireSV app or in the station's history.

It is an opportunity to connect tourism with research and environmental education.
